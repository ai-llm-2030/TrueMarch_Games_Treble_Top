How to play

501, double out. You start on 501 and count down. Three darts a visit. You must finish on exactly 0 with a double (the thin outer ring) or the bull.

Bust: going below 0, leaving 1, or reaching 0 without a double. The whole visit is wiped and the throw passes.

The board: the thin inner ring is a treble (×3), the outer ring a double (×2). The green 25 and the red bull (50) sit in the middle. Treble 20 (60) is the top of the shop.

Your nerves are in your arm. The aim drifts. Hold to breathe in: the drift settles. The ring round the aim is your breath; when it runs out, the arm shakes. Let go to throw.

The Checkout Lamp lights when you can finish: it shows the way out (e.g. T20 · T19 · D12) and outlines the bed for the next dart. Follow it in one visit for a lamp finish.

Big Mal's calls: a ton is 100+ in a visit; bed and breakfast is 26 (20, 5, 1); the madhouse is double 1; game shot is the winning double.
